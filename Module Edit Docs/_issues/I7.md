DECISION: Wix-first precedence could not apply to this module and the scope needs confirming. The Wix
    page, the entire Notion Interface III subtree and deck slides 49-62 and 311-323 are all I8 material:
    the Guyton curve, Pms, resistance to venous return. None of them covers the capillary boundary, the
    Starling forces, the revised Starling principle, compliance versus capacitance or the splanchnic
    reservoir. The module is therefore built from primary literature and fills a genuine hole, since
    nothing on the site previously explained where a fluid bolus goes.

    "Capillaries to Right Atrium" in its body. File 01 fixes the name as the latter.
