    ANDROMEDA-SHOCK-2 uses 13 percent. The page publishes the provenance of both rather than picking one.
    Decide whether the curriculum should state a single number.
NOTE: The threshold published is 13 percent from Michard 2000, PMID 10903232. The meta-analysis is Marik
    2009, PMID 19602972, 29 studies, 685 patients, PPV AUC 0.94, threshold 12.5 plus or minus 1.6
    percent. Messina 2023 is published as counterweight: 59 studies, 2,947 patients, AUC 0.77, threshold
    10.8 percent. The grey zone is Cannesson 2011 and Biais 2014.
NOTE: No human study of PPV validity in intra-abdominal hypertension exists. Only porcine data is given,
    Renner and Jacques, and the page states that human thresholds are unestablished.
