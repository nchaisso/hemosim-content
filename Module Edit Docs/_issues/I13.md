    both: "12 to 13 percent" as the traditional range and "13 percent" as the operational value,

NOTE: Page teaches 13 percent (Michard 2000, PMID 10903232) as the historical/operational threshold
    and 12.5 plus or minus 1.6 percent (Marik 2009, PMID 19602972, 29 studies, 685 patients, PPV AUC
    0.94) as the pooled mean. SVV is now explicitly taught with its own threshold, about 11.6 percent,
    rather than being folded into the PPV number. Messina 2023 (59 studies, 2,947 patients, AUC 0.77,
    threshold 10.8 percent) is the counterweight. Grey zone is Cannesson 2011 and Biais 2014.
NOTE: No human study of PPV validity in intra-abdominal hypertension exists. Only porcine data (Jacques
    2011, Renner 2009), and the page states human thresholds are unestablished.
